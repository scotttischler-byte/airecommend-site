Straight talk

What it can name, and what it can't.

Most tools in this category oversell. Here is the real mechanism.

It can

  • Name the business behind a visit from an office or corporate network
  • Show city and region for every visit
  • Show the full page-by-page path and time on each page
  • Attach a name and email when a visitor submits a form

It can't

  • Name a person browsing from home or a phone unless they identify themselves
  • Tell you who someone is from an IP address alone
  • Promise a match rate. It depends on who actually visits you

Questions

Before you ask.

Is this legal and private?

It is built to be consent-aware: it honors Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track, shows a consent banner by default (no ID or company name until a visitor accepts), links to a plain-language privacy page, and stores a salted hash instead of the raw IP address. People are only named when they submit a form themselves. You should still have a privacy notice on your site, and for legal advice on your situation, talk to your own attorney. We are not a law firm.

Will it slow my site?

No. It is a tiny script that loads asynchronously and sends small beacons in the background.

Who is it for?

Business owners who want to know who is looking, agencies that run many client sites, and sales teams that want to call the right company at the right moment.
